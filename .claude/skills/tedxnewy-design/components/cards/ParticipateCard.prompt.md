Photo card with a dark bottom scrim — the Participate row on the homepage and the image-led cards in the nav mega-menu.

```jsx
<ParticipateCard href="/volunteer" title="Volunteer with us"
  body="Six crews, year-round roles. No experience needed, just reliability and curiosity."
  image="/images/stage-dialogue.jpg" gradient="var(--grad-special)" />
```

Photo sits at 0.65 opacity under the scrim so white text always reads. Use `ratio="4/3"` for nav cards. Rows of three.

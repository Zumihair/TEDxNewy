Listing row for /events and /talks: compact photo left, text right, hairline divider supplied by the parent list. Title underlines itself left-to-right on hover.

```jsx
<EventRow href="/events/newcastle-2050-salon" image="/images/salon-2050/community-event.webp"
  label="Salon" labelAccent="red" title="Newcastle 2050"
  meta="30 April 2026 · Q Building, Honeysuckle"
  description="A room full of Novocastrians mapping the city they want in 25 years." />
```

Use for chronological lists on cream; use `PastEventCard` for the photo-led grids on dark sections.

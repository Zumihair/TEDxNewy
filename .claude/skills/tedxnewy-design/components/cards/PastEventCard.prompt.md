The past-event card used in rows of three on the dark maroon sections of the homepage, /salons and /signature.

```jsx
<PastEventCard href="/events/reframe-2025" image="/images/past-2025.jpg"
  date="18 October 2025" title="Reframe" subtitle="Newcastle City Hall" />
```

Designed for white-on-dark only. Rows of 3+ are a swipe carousel on mobile and a grid from tablet up. Leave `image` out and it renders `PhotoPending` rather than an empty gradient.

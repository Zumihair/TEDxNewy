The site's only card frame: white fill, 8px radius, hairline border, lifting 2px with a soft shadow on hover.

```jsx
<Card padding={24}><h3>Volunteer crews</h3><p>Six crews, year-round roles.</p></Card>
```

No coloured card fills, no left-border accents, no heavier radius. Photo-led cards use `ParticipateCard` or `PastEventCard` instead.

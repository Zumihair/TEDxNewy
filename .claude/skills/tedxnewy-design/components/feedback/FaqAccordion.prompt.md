Single-open FAQ list, hairline-divided. Opening one row closes the last; the row grows to its own height rather than snapping.

```jsx
<FaqAccordion faqs={[{ q: "Is there parking?", a: "Yes, the Honeysuckle car park is a two minute walk." }]} />
```

Lives on event pages, usually on the dark section near the ticket CTA. Questions are asked in the visitor's voice, answers are short and plain.
